# Contract & Handbook Clauses — Driver App Tracking

**Xtra Mile Couriers Ltd · Version 1.0 DRAFT · 13 July 2026**
**Status: DRAFT for director approval and solicitor review. Two clauses:
§1 goes in the staff handbook (employed drivers, lawful basis = legitimate
interests, so the handbook documents the monitoring); §2 goes in the
subcontractor agreement (lawful basis = contract, so it must be a term of
the contract itself).**

---

## 1. Staff handbook clause — Workplace monitoring: location tracking

> **Location tracking during shifts**
>
> The Company uses the TOM Driver App to run its same-day courier
> operation. While you are on shift, the app records your vehicle's GPS
> position and sends it to the Company's dispatch system. This is used
> for: allocating and tracking jobs, planning routes, giving customers
> accurate arrival times, evidencing service (including for medical
> deliveries), and driver safety.
>
> Tracking operates **only between starting and ending your shift** in the
> app. The app does not record your location off shift, and the Company's
> systems refuse location data outside a shift.
>
> Location data is retained for 90 days in raw form (a summarised route is
> kept with each job record) and is accessible to operations staff for the
> purposes above. It is not used as a general surveillance tool; any use
> of tracking data in a disciplinary process will be limited to the
> specific matter under investigation and you will be shown the data
> relied on.
>
> Full details, including your data-protection rights, are in the Driver
> Privacy Notice, which forms part of this handbook.

## 2. Subcontractor agreement clause — Tracking and data

> **X. Use of the Driver App; location data**
>
> X.1 The Subcontractor shall ensure that each driver performing Services
> uses the Company's Driver App and starts a shift in the App before
> accepting work. The App records the driver's GPS position **during
> active shifts only** and transmits it to the Company for job
> allocation, live tracking, route planning, customer arrival estimates
> and proof of service. Tracking of a driver outside an active shift does
> not occur.
>
> X.2 The Subcontractor shall bring the Company's Driver Privacy Notice to
> the attention of each driver before their first shift and shall not
> permit a driver to perform Services who has not received it.
>
> X.3 The Company shall process personal data of the Subcontractor's
> drivers (identity, location during shifts, proof-of-delivery records,
> and payment details for self-billing) as an independent controller, in
> accordance with UK GDPR and the Driver Privacy Notice. Retention
> periods are as set out in the Company's Data Retention Schedule.
>
> X.4 Proof-of-delivery records (signatures, photographs, scan events)
> created through the App belong to the Company and constitute the
> contractual record of performance of the Services.
>
> X.5 Where the parties agree in writing to narrow the scope of tracking
> for the Subcontractor's drivers, the Company shall configure the App
> accordingly; otherwise the standard scope in X.1 applies.

---

*Notes for the directors:* §2.X.3 deliberately makes the Company an
independent controller (not a processor for the subcontractor) — that
matches how the data is actually used. The per-contract narrowing in X.5
is supported in the software (per-driver tracking scope is declared in
TOM, never assumed).
