## What this notice covers

The TOM Driver App you use for your work: job assignment, live tracking,
parcel scanning, proof of delivery, messages with the office, and your pay
information.

## What we collect and why

| What | When | Why (purpose) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your GPS location (position, speed, heading, accuracy, battery) | **Only while you are on shift** — between "Start shift" and "End shift"/"Going home". Never off-shift; the app takes no readings and the server refuses any | Live job tracking, dispatch decisions, route planning, customer ETAs, proof of service | Legitimate interests (employed drivers); your contract (subcontractors) |
| Proof of delivery: signatures, photos, barcode scans (time and place) | At collection and delivery | Evidence the job was done, resolving disputes | Contract / legitimate interests |
| Job and shift history | While you work | Running the business, your pay | Contract |
| Pay, expenses, and (subcontractors) bank and tax details | When you provide them | Paying you; HMRC obligations | Contract / legal obligation |
| Messages between you and the office | When sent | Operations record | Legitimate interests |
| Device and login records (device ID, sign-ins, app actions) | When you use the app | Security, fraud prevention, audit | Legitimate interests |

## What we do NOT do

- We do **not** track you off shift. This is technically impossible in the
  app, not just a policy.
- We do **not** sell your data or use it for advertising.
- We do **not** make automated decisions about you with legal or similar
  effect — job allocation is decided by a human dispatcher.

## Who sees it

Operations staff see your live position and job progress on the dispatch
board while you're on shift. Customers see only the estimated arrival time
for their job — never your position feed. Push notifications are delivered
via Google Firebase; route planning uses Google Maps — both under Google's
data-processing terms. We do not otherwise share your data unless the law
requires it.

## How long we keep it

| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Raw GPS readings | 90 days, then deleted (a summarised per-job route is kept with the job record) |
| Job records, proof of delivery | 6 years (legal limitation period) |
| Pay and tax records | 6 years (HMRC) |
| Messages | 12 months |
| Your profile | While you work with us, then per the above periods |

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it (where
we don't have to keep it), to restrict or object to processing, and to
receive your data in a portable format. The app's **Privacy & data** screen
sends access and erasure requests straight to the office; or contact us
directly. We respond within one month.

You can withdraw location consent in the app at any time. Because live
tracking is part of how same-day courier work is dispatched, we would then
need to discuss with you how (or whether) work can be allocated — but
withdrawing is never treated as misconduct.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the
Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk · 0303 123 1113.
