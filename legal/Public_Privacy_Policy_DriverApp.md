## About this app

The TOM Driver App is used only by couriers working for or with Xtra Mile
Couriers Ltd ("we"). It is not a consumer app. If you don't drive for us,
the app will not do anything for you and collects nothing about you.

## What the app collects

- **Location** — precise GPS position, **only during an active shift**
  (between "Start shift" and "End shift" in the app). The app collects no
  location off shift; our servers reject location data outside a shift.
  In the store build, location may be collected in the background during a
  shift so tracking continues while you navigate or your screen is off.
- **Proof of delivery** — signatures, delivery photos you take, and
  barcode scans (with time and place).
- **Work records** — jobs, shifts, messages with our operations team, and
  your pay information (for subcontractors this includes bank details for
  paying you).
- **Device and security data** — device identifier for sign-in binding,
  login and app-action records, and a push-notification token.

## Why

To run a same-day courier operation: assigning jobs, live tracking and
customer arrival estimates, route planning, proof of service, paying you,
and keeping accounts secure. Legal bases under UK GDPR: performance of a
contract, our legitimate interests in running the business, and legal
obligations (tax records).

## What we don't do

No advertising, no selling of data, no data brokers, no tracking outside
shifts, no automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effect.

## Sharing

Operations staff see your live position and job progress during shifts.
Customers see only estimated arrival times. Service providers: Google
Firebase delivers push notifications; Google Maps provides routing — each
processes limited data under Google's data-processing terms. Otherwise we
share data only where the law requires.

## Retention

Raw GPS: 90 days. Job and proof-of-delivery records: 6 years. Pay and tax
records: 6 years. Messages: 12 months. Full schedule available on request.

## Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and portability —
request in-app (Privacy & data screen) or by contacting us. You may
complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

## Account deletion

Drivers who stop working with us can request account deletion in-app
(Privacy & data → erasure request) or by contacting us; we delete or
anonymise everything not legally required to be kept and confirm within
one month.
